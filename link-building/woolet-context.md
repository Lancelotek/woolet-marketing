   Tom Davies) starts far higher; most "custom" D2C brands only swap lens options.

   screenshot-able. Great for "AI in fashion/retail" stories.

   zero inventory waste. Fits sustainable-fashion listicles and journalist queries.

## Qualification rules (a prospect must pass ALL)

1. **Relevance** — page is about eyewear, fashion, sustainability, luxury goods,
   e-commerce/D2C, or AI-in-retail. Nothing else.
2. **Link intent evidence** — links to ≥1 competitor above, OR maintains a tools/brands
   list, OR cites an outdated/dead resource, OR mentions Woolet without a link.
3. **Editorial reachability** — a named author, editor, or working contact route.
4. **Quality floor** — real organic traffic signals; no link farms, no "write for us
   $50" pages, no PBN footprints (skip anything that sells links openly).
5. **Language/market** — EN first (US/UK/EU), PL second.

## Hard guardrails (Google link-spam compliance)

- **Never** buy links or offer money for a followed link. If a site is pay-to-play,
  the only acceptable route is a disclosed affiliate/partner placement (`rel=sponsored`).
- **Never** promise reciprocal links ("link to me and I'll link to you").
- No exact-match anchor-text requests — suggest brand or natural anchors only.
- Outreach is **always drafted, never auto-sent**. Human approves every email.
- Max 15 new outreach drafts per run; personalize every one from the page itself.
