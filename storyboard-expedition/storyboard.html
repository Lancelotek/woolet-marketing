Character reference - “the specimen”

  • Male, ~45, genuinely broad/large face, strong jaw, well-groomed full beard, short hair. Dignified, never a caricature.
  • Wardrobe: navy blazer over a plain grey crew tee - same outfit in every shot.
  • Demeanor: calm, dignified, a little weary - a wildlife subject unaware of the camera.
  • This image = first-frame / reference for every image-to-video generation (Veo 3 / Kling 2.x / Runway Gen-3 / Sora).
  • The board frames serve as keyframes - generate 5–8 s silent clips and cut to the VO.

Production notes

Music & SFX

Nature-doc bed: light strings + soft woodwinds. Cool/sparse S1–5, warm swell from S6, gentle resolve S10–11. VO always on top, music ducked −12/−14 dB under VO. SFX: fluorescent hum (1–5), snap (5), chime (6), workshop tools (7), sub-drop (8).

Grade - two acts

Act 1 (S1–5): cool, desaturated, greenish fluorescent. Act 2 (S6–11): warm, saturated, golden. The color flip happens on camera in S6 as he raises the phone. Brand accents on graphics only: gold #CAA449 · black #080807 · cream #EDE7D9.

Text & captions

Cormorant Garamond (display) + Barlow (small caps). Problem labels bottom-left like documentary field notes (S2–5); product labels center-lower (S6–9). Burned-in captions for the whole film (silent autoplay). End card holds the CTA 3+ s.
